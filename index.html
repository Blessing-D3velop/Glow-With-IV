The Science

What is IV Vitamin Therapy?

IV Vitamin Therapy is the process of administering medically approved vitamins & minerals directly into the bloodstream — guaranteeing 100% absorption.

Homeopathic medicine is derived from natural substances like plants and minerals, which stimulate the body's healing process while optimising absorption into the bloodstream.

It's important to note: IV drip fluids are generally classified as isotonic, hypotonic or hypertonic — this describes their level of concentration or electrolyte balance.
  • Isotonic — the same electrolyte concentration as blood plasma; doesn't move fluid in or out of cells.
  • Hypotonic — causes water to move into cells.
  • Hypertonic — causes water to move out of cells.
